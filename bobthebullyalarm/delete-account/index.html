Bob 😈 Privacy

Delete Your Bob Account

For Bob the Bully Alarm

You can delete your Bob account in the app or request deletion by email if you cannot access the app. Account deletion is permanent.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Bob and go to Settings.
  2. Open Danger Zone, then choose Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the address associated with your Bob account. Include the account email and the words “Bob account deletion request” in your message so we can identify the account.

Do not include your password or payment details. We may ask for information needed to verify that you own the account.

Email a deletion request

You can also email privacy@bobapp.dev.

Data deleted

After we verify and complete a request, we delete the Bob sign-in account and associated cloud data, including the profile and streak data, synced session history, achievements, generated Bob text pack, and cloud voice usage counts. The in-app deletion flow also clears app preferences and generated audio cached on that device.

An email request cannot remove data stored only on your device. After requesting deletion, uninstall Bob or clear its app data on each device where it was used.

Data that may remain

Google Play and Apple manage their own purchase records and subscriptions. Deleting your Bob account does not cancel a subscription or delete the store's transaction records. Cancel a subscription separately through the store where you purchased it. We may retain information only where required for legal, security, or platform reasons.

Questions

For help with a deletion request, contact privacy@bobapp.dev.
